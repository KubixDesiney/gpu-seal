# Code of Conduct

GPU-SEAL welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds and
levels of experience. We expect everyone to be respectful, factual, and
careful with provider and researcher privacy.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats,
intimidation, doxxing, sharing credentials or private provider material,
pressuring someone to run an unapproved experiment, and presenting uncertain
measurements as accusations.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed
to protect people, the project, or third parties. Report conduct concerns
privately through the repository's maintainer/security channel; do not include
provider secrets or raw unknown memory in a report.
